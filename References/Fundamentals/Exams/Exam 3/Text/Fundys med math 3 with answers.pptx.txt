### Slide 4
Single doses
 prescription should then indicate how many doses the client will receive throughout the day
 Most often every 4, 6, 8 , or 12 hours (q4, q6, etc...) 
 Divide total daily by how many times a day client will receive...so:
Q4 = 6 times a day (24 hours divided by every 4 hours = 6)
Q6 = 4 times a day
Q8 = 3 times a day
Q12 = 2 times a day
 So example from last slide = 1,018mg/day
 How many mg would be delivered per dose if ordered Q6? What about Q12? Round to nearest whole number
1/28/26
Q6 = 1,018/4 = 255mg
Q12 = 1,018/2 = 509mg
If doing dimensional analysis you can add this into the equation:
- add 1 day over 24 hours and ___ hours over 1dose

### Slide 8
CONSIDERATIONS
 What needs to be in prescription
Date and time prescription written 
Client’s full name
Name of medication
Indication (reason)
Dose: amount times or frequency of administration and strength 
Route of admin
Any specific instructions (ex. Take with food or without)
Signature
 Abbreviations to avoid:
QD – once daily but looks like QID (4 times daily)
HS – could mean bedtime or half-strength
DC – could mean discharge or discontinue 
 What would you do if you have a prescription missing a piece or with one of the above abbreviations?
