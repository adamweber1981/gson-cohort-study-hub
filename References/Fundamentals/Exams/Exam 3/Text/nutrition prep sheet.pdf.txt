Nutrition
Read this before class — it will make the lecture click faster.



  Big picture: Nutrition is foundational to healing, immunity, and recovery. Nurses assess nutritional
  status, implement prescribed diets, manage dysphagia safely, and coordinate enteral and parenteral
  nutrition — not just deliver trays.



  BMI & NUTRITIONAL NEEDS

  • BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²)

  • Underweight <18.5 · Normal 18.5–24.9 · Overweight 25–29.9 · Obese ≥30

  • BMI is a screening tool — combine with clinical assessment

  • Caloric needs: 25–35 kcal/kg/day; more if critically ill

  • Protein needs: 0.8–1.0 g/kg/day; more if critically ill or healing a wound

  DYSPHAGIA & ASPIRATION

  • Signs of dysphagia: coughing while eating, voice change after swallowing, regurgitation, abnormal
     tongue/mouth movements
  • Silent aspiration: no cough — but O₂ drops or recurrent pneumonia

  • Consult speech therapy for swallow study (IDDSI framework)

  • Sit upright 90° for all meals; maintain 30–60 min after eating

  • Never upgrade or downgrade diet without an order; educate family — a well-meaning visitor
     bringing the wrong food is a real safety risk


  ENTERAL & PARENTERAL NUTRITION

  • Enteral (NG tube, G-tube): through GI tract; check placement before each feeding; HOB elevated;
     check gastric residuals; monitor for diarrhea, hyperglycemia, tube occlusion
  • Parenteral (TPN/PPN): IV through central line when GI tract cannot be used; goal is to transition
     enteral → oral
  • Enteral is always preferred over parenteral when GI tract is functional




  SPECIAL DIETS & DISEASE

  • Low sodium (<2 g/day): heart failure, hypertension, CKD, cirrhosis

  • Renal diet: restrict K⁺, phosphorus, sodium, protein, fluid

  • Diabetic: consistent carbs 45–60 g/meal; coordinate meals with insulin

  • Cancer/chemo: high calorie/protein; address nausea; manage mucositis

  • Never force dietary compliance — educate, discuss, document refusals, notify dietitian

  • Always respect cultural and religious food practices
