Study Hub Changelog

Updates to practice exams, references, and site features.

Modern Workspace and five themes

October 31, 2026
  • A cleaner course dashboard with desktop sidebar and compact mobile navigation.
  • Choose two light or three dark themes in Settings. Your selection also applies to shared-engine practice exams.
  • The original Goldfarb logo and handwritten Cohort One. Second to None. slogan follow your theme colors. The logo is centered on mobile.
  • Courses remain ordered by recent exam updates; only the newest exam starts expanded. Topic/subtopic selection and custom question counts remain available.
  • References now focuses on course source materials; the Question Bank Review panel has been removed.
  • Existing question banks, scoring, feedback, and saved answer-color preferences are unchanged.

Pharmacology Exam 3: stronger answer choices

October 29, 2026
  • More plausible multiple-choice and select-all-that-apply alternatives help you distinguish related medication concepts.
  • SATA now varies from two to four correct selections. It still uses all-or-nothing scoring.
  • All 278 questions remain available, with the same category/drug selectors and question-count controls.
  • Answer explanations clarify important distinctions. Question and answer order remain randomized.

Pharmacology Exam 3 — New Practice Bank and Complete References

October 29, 2026
  • Added 278 practice questions, with 10–11 direct questions for each medication entry in the Exam 3 prototype list. Generated questions and medication selectors use generic names.
  • Choose Mixed practice for 50 questions, or Custom practice to combine categories and drug/class subtopics and choose the number of questions. Multi-drug matching appears only when all its drugs are selected.
  • Added all 11 original course-folder sources to the exam reference collection, with GitHub links and an in-page viewer or download option. The narrated presentation is available as a reconstructed, integrity-checked original download.
  • The mixed allocation is a study plan, not the instructor's exam weighting.
  • Production updates are available on the GitHub Pages Study Hub. The Render test site is separate. If an installed app shows an older version, reload it; reinstalling may be necessary and can clear local preferences or an in-progress attempt.

Study Hub Analytics — Launch and Verification

October 19, 2026
  • Launched a public Study Hub Analytics dashboard with reporting periods of 7 days, 30 days, and all time.
  • Integrated Cloudflare Web Analytics into deployed HTML pages for site traffic reporting.
  • Added anonymous exam engagement tracking for Health Assessment Exam 2 Versions 1–8 and Fundamentals Exam 2 Versions 1–8, covering starts, scoring actions, completed attempts, resets, reference opens, and PDF downloads.
  • Deployed a Cloudflare Worker and D1 database to collect and aggregate exam events without collecting student identities.
  • Configured GitHub Actions to inject tracking into the 16 supported exams and connect the dashboard to the analytics service automatically during deployment.
  • Resolved tracking configuration issues and confirmed successful event submissions and dashboard updates.
  • Configured score reporting to count completed attempts only after all 50 questions are answered and the exam is scored; mean and median remain hidden until five completed attempts have been recorded for that exam version.
  • Next steps: verify full-exam completion and score recording, add collapsible course and exam sections to the dashboard, and extend automatic engagement tracking and categorization to future exams. New exams currently receive page-view analytics, but not automatic detailed exam engagement tracking.
  • Testing note: ad blockers may prevent analytics requests. Test scores can be cleared from Cloudflare D1, but aggregate records cannot distinguish test attempts from student attempts, so cleanup must be performed carefully.
